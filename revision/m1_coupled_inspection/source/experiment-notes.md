## Planning and safety

The body reference is obtained by a three-dimensional positional search over the surveyed collision map. The multi-plane seed search explores body offsets, heading and shape, then the V2 optimizer jointly varies cubic B-spline control points and nonuniform span durations. A separate geometry instance validates the candidate using the SUPER airframe, landing gear, flexible arm and tool envelope. An infeasible full route may yield a certified prefix. The aircraft follows that prefix and holds its achieved position. Register planning wall time from the beginning of the body search through spline validation. A single horizon spans the inspection task in M1.

Straight constrains the two shape coordinates to zero bend. Fixed maintains 80 degrees and a fixed bend plane after prepositioning. AET-Seq. adapts the body-first decomposition to this SUPER/Qingyu platform. The method fixes the aircraft reference and optimizes shape in its remaining corridor. ## Sensors and physical measurements

All views use the same compiled industrial MuJoCo geometry and geom-group mask. ORB-SLAM3 receives each wrist RGB-D exposure after a registered 0.20 s delay. The target is an optical inspection pose. The registered face is viewed at a nominal 168 mm distance. The digital aperture has 670 mm of vertical space. The loaded straight apparatus measures about 659 mm in the surveyed upright configuration. The two required 60 mm clearances exceed the remaining vertical space in that configuration.

The physics integrator runs at 0.5 ms, PX4 HIL at 4 ms, control and the independent clearance audit at 50 Hz and the wrist camera at 25 Hz. Contact is counted at the physics integration step. The synchronized ULog and ROS bag preserve flight-estimator and motor data. Rigid component meshes and analytic primitives are checked with a conservative geometry broad phase and signed convex GJK/EPA refinements. Each lower/upper interval bounds the sample-wise minimum. Figure curves use 5 Hz exact geometry evaluations. The self-separation is a separate, positive-gap requirement. The 60 mm threshold is for external obstacles.

The arm-root reaction moment is recovered from the loaded native model with logged positions, velocities and actuator controls. Its spatial wrench is transferred from the subtree centre of mass to the arm mount. The gravitational moment is independently computed from the total horizontal centre-of-mass offset. The recorded front–rear thrust difference is the sum of the front-right and front-left motor thrusts minus the two rear thrusts. Success requires zero physical contacts, six-centimetre external clearance across the audited flight, positive verified self clearance, and concurrent tool position within 35 mm and axis within 3 degrees for at least one second. The flange must lie in the wrist image at 120–180 mm optical depth. The native depth image independently confirms the visible face for at least one second. ## Reproduction and records

On the NUC, the project checkout supplies the V2 planner, native PX4 build, ORB-SLAM3 runner and frozen sensing sources. Add the M1 source directory, existing revision/scripts, the V2 package and the MuJoCo environment to PYTHONPATH, and source ROS Noetic. Use the committed scene/ directory, then run m1_campaign.py in a fresh result folder. Each flight file is checked before a resume. The exposure index pairs native RGB and depth digests with the exact state digest at exposure. m1_records.py verifies all exposures and packages the compact sensor and flight logs. m1_media.py renders the same states and reads the original wrist images. m1_plates.py and m1_report.py use the figures and compact local archive to prepare the review.
