Overall framework of morphology-aware rolling whole-body planning. Simplified mechanical reconstruction from the supplied hardware photographs and assembly references. The underbody actuator supports one constant-curvature section; the distal D435 and gripper form a rigid assembly whose optical axis points in the grasp direction and rotates with the distal assembly. Four short surface-strain sensors are distributed circumferentially; only the visible sensors are depicted. Offline motion-capture supervision trains a fixed strain predictor. Online eye-in-hand visual observations are compensated for UAV base motion and fused with strain observations using tracking-dependent confidence. Local whole-body seeds are refined jointly in vehicle motion, morphology, and timing. Independent numerical validation and handoff authorization precede execution; rejected candidates trigger replanning. C1 denotes the direct-handoff tolerance gate, while quintic C2 bridges are revalidated before use. The planning benchmark used a second-order arm-response model through the common morphology-state interface; sensing was evaluated separately on recorded data. The diagram describes the architecture and does not assert an experimentally demonstrated end-to-end hardware loop.
